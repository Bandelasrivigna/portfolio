The interesting part of each one isn't the feature list. It's the failure it was designed to survive.

Backend · Retrieval & extraction

Grounded Retrieval and Extraction Service

A service that turns unstructured documents into structured records over a REST endpoint, with a confidence flag on each field so a reviewer knows what to check rather than having to trust the whole output.

  • Cached embeddings and batched the outbound calls, which brought latency and cost per document down far enough for the service to run on every upload rather than on request.
  • Structured as stages with a validation gate between each, so a malformed or unsupported result was stopped at the boundary instead of returned to the caller.
  • Covered with pytest against inputs with known correct answers, so every change was measured against the same baseline rather than a fresh impression.

Full-stack · Healthcare

Samaa

A three-application counseling platform on a shared PostgreSQL backend: a React Native student mobile app, a Next.js clinical dashboard for counselors and therapists, and an internal admin panel.

  • Access control enforced entirely in the database through Row Level Security across fourteen tables, with per-conversation policies restricting counselors to their own assigned sessions, so a fault in any client cannot expose what it should not reach.
  • Realtime chat over Supabase WebSockets with a shared counselor queue and optimistic claim handling to resolve simultaneous accepts, plus voice and scheduled video sessions through the 100ms SDK.
  • Append-only clinical records with automatic time-based retention and audited privileged access, plus admin tooling for institution onboarding, CSV ingestion and per-term PDF compliance reports carrying aggregate counts only.

Three clients, one Postgres, access enforced by policy not by client
